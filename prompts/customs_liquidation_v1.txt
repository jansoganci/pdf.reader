Read the customs liquidation.
The document number is the liquidation number printed beside "N° ET DATE LIQUIDATION". Do not put the declaration number in that field.
The document date is the date on that same liquidation line. Do not use the declaration date.
The customs value is the printed article value (VALEUR). Do not calculate it.
The tax amount is the single tax line whose code is 002109. Do not use the sous-total or the payment total as the tax.
The total amount is the payment total printed on the recap. Keep it separate from the tax line.
Copy each printed tax line as a line. Do not copy a total row as a line.
