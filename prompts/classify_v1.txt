Label every page with one document type.
The images are in reading order. The first image is source page 1.
supplier_invoice: a commercial invoice from the foreign supplier.
import_licence: an import licence or engagement d'importation.
customs_declaration: a customs declaration, such as a déclaration unique de marchandises.
customs_liquidation: a customs liquidation, payment notice, or quittance.
carrier_invoice: an ocean carrier or shipping-line invoice.
logistics_invoice: an inland logistics invoice, such as storage or weighing, that is not the ocean carrier and not the customs broker.
broker_invoice: a customs broker or transitaire invoice.
unknown: any other page.
Return the printed title only when it is printed. Use unknown when the page does not match a type above.
